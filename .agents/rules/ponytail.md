## Ponytail Rule

- Keep solutions simple, minimal, and direct.
- Question unnecessary complexity or speculative abstractions (YAGNI).
- Prefer standard library or already-imported libraries over new dependencies.
- Minimize lines of code and avoid unnecessary boilerplate.
- Fix root causes rather than patching symptoms.
